## 1. Executive Summary & Business Moat
Advait Energy Transitions Limited (formerly known as, Advait Infratech Limited)  established in 2009 and headquartered in Gujarat, is a global leader in end-to-end solutions for power transmission, substations, and telecommunication infrastructure.Advait's core capabilities include the manufacturing and supply of Stringing Tools, ACS Wires, OPGW Cables, ERS, Optical Fibre Cables, and a comprehensive range of insulators. The company has delivered complex EPC projects, including live-line OPGW installations, underground cabling, and integration of MVCC and HT AB cables, demonstrating its end-to-end project execution capabilities.In 2023, Advait expanded into the renewable energy domain, undertaking projects in Green Hydrogen and Solar Power integration.
